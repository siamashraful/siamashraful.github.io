<div class="prose" markdown="1">

I'm Siam — an **Investment Analyst at Invest Nova Scotia**, working with
innovative startups and high-growth companies across the province, primarily in
ICT and AI.

My job runs from first contact to final decision: sourcing companies through
founders, accelerators, and venture investors; diligencing business models,
market opportunity, competitive positioning, financials, and management teams;
and writing the investment committee memorandums that carry a recommendation to
senior leadership. Around that core, I spend a lot of time in market research —
AI, software, and the emerging sectors where Nova Scotia can build real
competitive advantage.

## The path here

**Dalhousie University** gave me the engineering foundation — a BSc in Computer
Science (2024) and two shipped builds: a real-time library seat reservation system
in Unity and an Android marketplace app with Firebase and GPS listings.

**TSX Trust** was my first contact with capital markets infrastructure.
Maintaining shareholder records taught me that behind every clean financial
statement is a messy operational reality — and that data integrity is a daily
practice, not a feature.

**Outlier AI** taught me structured judgment: scoring AI outputs against rubrics
and writing rationales sharp enough that someone else could reproduce the call.

**RBC** put those instincts to work at banking scale — tracing Critical Data
Elements through reporting and origination systems, interpreting SQL and COBOL
transformation logic, and packaging it all into documentation auditors could
trust.

**Invest Nova Scotia** is where it converges. Due diligence is data lineage
applied to companies: don't trust a number until you know where it came from —
whether it's a revenue figure, a market size, or a growth story.

## Beyond the deals

I care about the ecosystem as much as the portfolio. Conferences, industry
events, founder coffees — I try to contribute to an environment where Nova
Scotia startups can access the capital, resources, and networks they need to
scale. The [coverage section](/#coverage) of this site is where my own published
analysis will land, openly and dated.

## Outside the terminal

I experiment with emerging AI tools, sketch interface ideas in Figma, and
occasionally fall down startup-concept rabbit holes of my own. Accessibility and
sustainability keep showing up in what I choose to build.

If any of this overlaps with what you're working on — [say hello](/contact/).
