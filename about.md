<div class="prose">

I'm Siam — a computer science graduate working as a **Data Analyst on RBC's Data
Traceability & Controls team** in Halifax, Nova Scotia.

My job, in one sentence: when a number shows up in a bank's reporting, I can tell
you where it was born, every system it passed through, and what could have gone
wrong along the way. I document Critical Data Elements, build lineage diagrams,
and work with metadata teams to get that knowledge into Collibra where auditors
and engineers can actually use it.

## The path here

**Dalhousie University** gave me the engineering foundation — a BSc in Computer
Science (2024) and two shipped builds: a real-time library seat reservation system
in Unity and an Android marketplace app with Firebase and GPS listings.

**TSX Trust** was my first contact with capital markets infrastructure. Maintaining
shareholder records taught me that behind every clean financial statement is a
messy operational reality — and that data integrity is a daily practice, not a
feature.

**Outlier AI** taught me structured judgment: scoring AI outputs against rubrics
and writing rationales sharp enough that someone else could reproduce the call.

**RBC** is where it converges — banking data, regulatory stakes, and the
discipline of tracing claims back to their source.

## Where this is heading

The same instinct that makes me good at lineage — *don't trust a number until you
know where it came from* — is the foundation of investment analysis. I'm building
toward capital markets work deliberately: the [coverage section](/#coverage) of
this site is where my research will land as it's completed, openly and dated.

## Outside the terminal

I experiment with emerging AI tools, sketch interface ideas in Figma, and
occasionally fall down startup-concept rabbit holes that merge analytics with
design. Accessibility and sustainability keep showing up in what I choose to
build.

If any of this overlaps with what you're working on — [say hello](/contact/).
